How the call is made

PlowLots and driveways when the blended forecast reaches 1.5" or more in the next 24 hours. The board shows the hour the 1.5" mark is expected so you can stage trucks.
ShovelSidewalk crews for anything from a trace up to 2". From 1.5" to 2" both run: trucks on lots, crews on walks. Over 2", walks need a blower or sidewalk machine instead of hand shovels.
Sand / saltAny freezing rain or drizzle, ice accumulation, snow under 0.5", or a thaw then refreeze across 32°F. Below 15°F rock salt barely works, so the board says sand.

The main number is the National Weather Service Anchorage forecast, which forecasters adjust by hand for local terrain like the Hillside and Eagle River valley. Four global models (European, American GFS, Canadian, German) are shown beside it. When they agree, confidence is high. When they spread out, watch radar and check again before committing crews.
